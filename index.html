Hi, I'm the egg behind the art 🥚

I'm a self-taught artist with a deep love for creatures great and small. Whether it's your beloved cat curled up by the window, a grumpy little frog, or a fantastical forest beast — I love bringing animals to life with bold colour, warmth, and a whole lot of personality.

Fresh Egg Designs started as a way to share that joy with the world. Every piece is made with real care — from hand-painted acrylic originals and open-edition prints, to illustrated bookmarks, wearable art, and one-of-a-kind custom commissions.

Commissions

Let's make something made just for you

✦ Commissions open

Want a one-of-a-kind portrait of your pet, a custom creature, or something uniquely yours? I'd love to hear about it! Commissions are currently open — reach out via Instagram or Etsy to get the conversation started. I work in acrylic and digitally, and love a fun brief.

01

Get in touch

Send me a message on Instagram or Etsy with your idea and any reference photos you love.

02

We chat details

We'll talk through size, style, medium, timeline, and pricing until we're both excited.

03

I get painting

I work my magic. You'll get progress updates so you can watch your piece come to life.

04

It's yours

Your finished piece ships to your door, or your digital files arrive — ready to treasure forever.

Say Hello 👋
